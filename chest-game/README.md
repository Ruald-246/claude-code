# 🎮 Mystic Chest Hunter

A web-based chest opening game where you collect items of varying rarity and build your fortune!

## 🎯 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Start with 100 gold
3. Purchase and open chests to receive random items
4. Sell items for gold or keep them for your collection
5. Unlock achievements and complete collections

## 📦 Chest Types

### Wooden Chest (📦)
- **Cost:** 10 gold
- **Items:** 1-2 items
- **Best for:** Starting out and building initial gold

### Bronze Chest (🎁)
- **Cost:** 50 gold
- **Items:** 2-3 items
- **Best for:** Mid-game progression

### Silver Chest (💎)
- **Cost:** 200 gold
- **Items:** 3-4 items
- **Best for:** Better item quality

### Golden Chest (👑)
- **Cost:** 1,000 gold
- **Items:** 4-5 items
- **Best for:** High-value items and rare finds

### Diamond Chest (✨)
- **Cost:** 5,000 gold
- **Items:** 5-7 items
- **Best for:** Legendary and Mythic items

## 🎨 Item Rarities

1. **Common** (Gray) - 1x value multiplier
2. **Uncommon** (Green) - 2x value multiplier
3. **Rare** (Blue) - 5x value multiplier
4. **Epic** (Purple) - 10x value multiplier
5. **Legendary** (Orange) - 25x value multiplier
6. **Mythic** (Gold) - 100x value multiplier ✨

## 🗡️ Item Types

- **Weapons** ⚔️ - Swords, Bows, Staffs, Daggers, and more
- **Armor** 🛡️ - Helmets, Chestplates, Boots, Shields
- **Potions** 🧪 - Health, Mana, Strength, Speed potions
- **Special** 🌟 - Pets, Mounts, Artifacts, Relics

## 🏆 Achievements

- **First Steps** - Open your first chest
- **Enthusiast** - Open 10 chests
- **Collector** - Open 50 chests
- **Master Hunter** - Open 100 chests
- **Wealthy** - Accumulate 10,000 gold
- **Legendary Find** - Find a Legendary item
- **Mythic Discovery** - Find a Mythic item
- **Arsenal** - Collect 10 different weapons
- **Fortune** - Earn 100,000 total value

## 💡 Game Features

### Inventory Management
- View all your collected items
- Sort items by value
- Sell individual items or sell all at once
- Items organized by rarity with color-coded borders

### Collection Tracker
- Track unique items collected in each category
- Progress bars show completion percentage
- Hundreds of unique item combinations to discover

### Auto-Save
- Your progress is automatically saved to browser local storage
- Resume your game anytime by refreshing the page

## 🎮 Gameplay Tips

1. **Start Small:** Open Wooden or Bronze chests to build up gold
2. **Sell Wisely:** Keep rare items for collection, sell commons for gold
3. **Work Your Way Up:** Save gold for higher-tier chests for better items
4. **Complete Collections:** Try to collect all unique item combinations
5. **Watch for Mythics:** Diamond chests have the best chance for Mythic items!
